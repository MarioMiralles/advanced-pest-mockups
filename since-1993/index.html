Services

What we can help with

From a few ants in the kitchen to termites in the door frame. If you have a pest we cannot treat, we will still give you guidance.

Termite inspections

Drywood or subterranean, we find where they are and how far they have gone. Spot little piles of frass that look like sawdust? Call us.

Termite treatment

Rodent control

Roof rats in the attic or mice in the garage. We find how they get in and help you shut the door on them.
